## 🎨 Design Features Applied to All Pages

### Visual Effects
- **Animated Background**: 3 floating gradient orbs with 25s animation cycles
- **Custom Cursor**: Dual-ring cursor with 100ms follower delay and hover effects
- **Glassmorphism**: Cards with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and semi-transparent backgrounds
- **3D Transforms**: Hover effects with `translateY()`, `scale()`, and `rotate()`
- **Gradient Animations**: Shifting background gradients on headings and CTAs
- **AOS Scroll Animations**: Fade-up effects with staggered delays

### Color Palette
- Primary: `#667eea` (purple)
- Secondary: `#f093fb` (pink)
- Accent: `#4facfe` (cyan)
- Success: `#10b981`
- Warning: `#f59e0b`
- Error: `#ef4444`
- Dark: `#0a0e27`

### Interactions
- **Button Ripple**: White circle expands from click point
- **Button Hover**: Lift up 5px, scale 1.05x, shadow expansion
- **Card Hover**: Lift up 10px, scale 1.02x, border glow
- **Input Focus**: Border glow, shadow expansion, background brightening
- **Drag & Drop**: Background color change, border color shift, scale transform

### Tool-Specific Features
All 5 verification tools include:
- Floating tool navigation bar (always visible)
- Drag & drop file/text upload
- File preview (image/video/audio player)
- Animated loading states with spinners
- Color-coded result scores:
  - **High** (>70%): Red - AI-generated likely
  - **Medium** (40-70%): Orange - Uncertain
  - **Low** (<40%): Green - Human-generated likely
- Smooth result animations with AOS
- Mobile responsive design

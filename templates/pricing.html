Frequently Asked Questions

How do verifications work? ▼
Each verification consumes 1 credit from your monthly allowance. Text, image, video, audio, and URL checks each count as one verification.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? ▼
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
What happens if I exceed my monthly limit? ▼
You can purchase additional credit packages at any time. Or wait until your monthly credits reset on your renewal date.
Is there a free trial for paid plans? ▼
Yes! Individual plan subscribers get a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required to start.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept all major credit/debit cards, PayPal, and UPI (for India). Payments are securely processed through Razorpay.
